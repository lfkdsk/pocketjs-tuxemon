# Dojo integration and radio font deduplication

## Result

The reviewed Spyder Dojo implementation is integrated on the current game
baseline without regressing the later radio, deterministic `update_time`,
exact character speeds, coverage repairs or seamless outdoor exits. All three
`dojo_method` uses and both `change_taste` uses are now Native. The resulting
import executes 13,355 of 13,617 action uses (98.1%) and 8,444 of 8,663
condition uses (97.5%), with no Placeholder uses.

The radio frequency readout now reuses the existing 18 px `text-lg` atlas.
The candidate pak contains `ui:font.3` and no `ui:font.5`. On the same combined
source tree this reduces `dist/main.pak` by 2,057,440 bytes and the bilingual
Web pak by 8,167,200 bytes. Both tuner resolutions were regenerated and opened
at 3x nearest-neighbour scale; the `94.7` readout remains readable and
unclipped.

The current-main save fixture was generated by an untouched checkout of the
current baseline, not by relabelling candidate bytes. It loads, resaves under
the candidate identity and continues on the current tape. All five maintained
mainline terminal states are unchanged. Deterministic import, complete game
and component tests, every CI shell command, full GB6 multi-rate replay, Web,
English and Chinese PSP, and interleaved real-QuickJS J3/J4 gates all pass.

## Dojo behavior and coverage

The production runtime provides the reviewed behavior in English and Chinese:

- the two students devolve stage1/stage2 monsters to a qualifying earlier
  history form while preserving identity, nickname, level, experience, tastes,
  IVs, training points, status and moves; absolute HP is clamped to the new
  maximum and the form is registered as caught;
- Xiang forgets one known move and offers unknown moveset rows at or below the
  monster's level, including the direct one-candidate path;
- Zhu chooses another cold/warm taste by upstream rarity weights, recalculates
  stats, clamps HP and reports the old and new tastes;
- cancel, insufficient funds, an empty/stale target and no learnable move do
  not charge, do not show a false success line and leave the service retryable;
- successful changes survive the production save path.

The known authored-order limitation remains documented rather than hidden:
after one taste change, Zhu's second `yes` is a no-op without a charge because
the map reset fiber runs before the purchase fiber resamples its guard.
Upstream samples the guards together and would also reroll twice for one
payment; the port deliberately consumes the selection after one change.

## QuickJS performance

J3 (hospital cure through the radio broadcast) and J4 (radio broadcast through
the Data Center finale) were measured with the real `rquickjs` desktop host at
480x272. Each suite used fresh processes in the order current main -> candidate
-> candidate -> current main, with separate baseline/candidate roots and a
matching precompiled host for each worktree. `G6_PROFILE_FRAMES`,
`G6_BUDGET_MS` and `G6_STARTUP_MS` were unset. The accepted J3 batch recorded
one-minute load 4.82-5.52; the accepted J4 batch started at 4.47-5.70.
