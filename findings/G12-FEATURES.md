# Title Battle demo transport and difficulty hardening

## Result

The title Battle no longer exposes the player-facing demo chapter menu while
its disposable battle session owns the screen. The same ownership rule now
covers ordinary game scenes, queued scene/battle handoffs, and both active and
settling Eclipse Park transactions. Returning to an ordinary world state
restores SELECT transport as before.

All five upstream difficulty recipes now have explicit team-size, level, and
candidate-pool assertions. The implementation remains game-owned: RPG Kit is
still `65280e9b` and PocketJS is still `862040bd`.

## Failure and chosen behavior

The demo overlay handles SELECT before the demo controller advances. Before
this fix, a title battle left the demo controller closed but still eligible to
open its chapter menu. Choosing a chapter replaced the temporary title
session; the title owner then detected that its battle scene had disappeared
and raised the fatal error `title battle: temporary battle scene was replaced
unexpectedly`.

The production bundle reproduced the original failure deterministically:

```text
before battle battle
menu true
title battle: temporary battle scene was replaced unexpectedly
```

The fix blocks the SELECT edge while another transaction owns temporary state
instead of silently abandoning that transaction. This matches the visible
control model: finish or leave the battle, game scene, or Park visit, then use
chapter transport. The same production sequence now stays in its battle and
never creates the demo menu:

```text
before battle battle
menu false
after no-error battle battle
```

The guard is centralized in `ui/game-overlay.tsx` and covers:

- an active reducer scene, including title and ordinary battles, PC, shop,
  storage, Dojo, daycare, radio, and other modal game scenes;
- a queued battle or queued scene during the one-frame ownership handoff;
- an active Eclipse Park visit;
- a Park summary waiting to settle.

An inactive, fully settled Park state and an ordinary world state still allow
SELECT. External automation hooks remain available for test and web controls;
the ownership rule applies to the player-facing menu entry that caused the
crash.

`tests/title-battle.test.ts` exercises every guarded state plus the two allowed
world states. `tools/feature-modes-visual-fixture.ts` performs the original
input sequence through the production `dist/main` bundle and publishes the
`titleBattleDemoBlocked` probe; `tests/feature-modes-visual.test.ts` requires
that probe while retaining all existing visual checks.

## Five upstream difficulty recipes

The source program in `mods/tuxemon/maps/battle_menu.yaml` creates equal player
and opponent parties with one additional `random_monster` command per tier.
Every generated monster in a tier uses its fixed authored level:

| Difficulty | Player team | Opponent team | Level |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Beginner | 1 | 1 | 10 |
| Easy | 2 | 2 | 15 |
| Normal | 3 | 3 | 20 |
| Hard | 4 | 4 | 25 |
| Expert | 5 | 5 | 30 |

The regression test invokes the production title runtime for all five rows. It
asserts both party sizes, every member's level, and membership in that tier's
imported candidate pool. This detects truncation on either side while keeping
the random species choice deterministic and data-backed.

Two mutations ran only in isolated worktrees:

| Mutation | Required failure |
|---|---|
| remove the composite SELECT ownership guard | ownership test expected the input to be consumed but received `false` |
| cap the title spawn loop at one member | Easy expected party sizes `[2, 2]` but received `[1, 1]` |

Both mutants failed and their worktrees were removed.

array made the ownership policy directly importable in Bun tests without
changing rendered nodes. The production visual suite matched all 20 committed
bilingual/two-resolution PNGs: 5 tests, 166 assertions, no golden change.

The English and Chinese title-battle frames at 480×272 and 960×544 were opened
at original resolution and inspected. Trainer and monster art, both HUDs,
party indicators, HP text and bars, dialog, and localized labels remain
visible and aligned; the 960×544 frames retain the intended nearest-neighbour
2× composition.

The real `dist/main` probe starts a title battle, presses SELECT, advances the
bundle, and confirms both that the battle remains active and that no demo menu
node appears. This is the fixed version of the player-visible crash path, not
only a reducer-unit simulation.
